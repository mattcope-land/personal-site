Starting from the company's original choice to name its AI product *Claude*, a clearly human name, Anthropic has consistently taken steps to anthropomorphize its software. I find this increasingly troubling, and worth paying attention to.

While most of the major AI companies go out of their way to *not* anthropomorphize their products, Anthropic does the opposite. Claude responds to @Claude in Slack, and joins web meetings as itself, encouraging people to perceive it as a *teammate* instead of *computer software*. Claude agents attribute authorship to "Claude" at every opportunity, and [Anthropic even weaves an invisible (to humans) signature into all text generated with Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark), in the name of "safety" of course.

In ways both subtle and obvious, Anthropic seems to be working hard to create a perception of *one Claude*, an independent entity, as opposed to various instances of computer software run in service of human operators.
