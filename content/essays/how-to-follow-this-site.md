To follow this site you can use the [RSS feed](https://mattcope.land/index.xml) and any feed aggregator you like.

I use [Readwise Reader](https://readwise.io/read). It’s a wonderful app with many capabilities beyond aggregating RSS feeds. It also costs about $10 per month.

[Inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com/) is another option with a “free forever” plan. I’ve tested it and it seems decent, though I haven’t used it extensively.

There are many RSS readers out there. These days you can also probably just vibe code your own.
